About our University

Our University was established in 1929 by a man named, Charles Creek and his wife Haven Creek in Jamestown NC. They started out with the total student population of 100 students. The school grew in popularity quickly due to our excellent academic programs and student employment. Now, we have a student body of 9,000 students and growing.

Some of the benefits of our University are:

  • Early entry masters programs.
  • Specialized tutors for each and every degree.
  • Etiquette training for future employment.
  • Specialized networking opportunities for each degree.
